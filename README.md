# Wuxi Tenco International Trading Co., Ltd.

Wuxi Tenco is a precision CNC machining manufacturer in Wuxi, China, founded in 2020. With a 5,000 sqm facility, 60+ staff and 70+ CNC machines, Tenco provides CNC milling, turning, drilling, wire EDM, sinker EDM and 3D printing for aerospace, defense, medical, robotics, semiconductor and industrial customers. AS9100D and ISO 9001 certified, with DFM review and quotation within 24 hours and no minimum order quantity.
